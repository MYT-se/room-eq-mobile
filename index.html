Before you start
  1. Save the DSP configuration. PEQ flat/bypassed on the speakers; keep crossovers and limiters ON.
  2. Plug the UMIK-1 into the iPhone (USB-C, or Lightning adapter). Turn the phone's volume low.
  3. Send the phone's audio to the system: AirPlay, Bluetooth or the system's own input. Mono signals: set the route to a single output.
  4. Quiet room. Mic at seated ear height, pointing up (90° file) or at the speakers (0° file).

2 · Check the level

Microphone off.
Raise the phone/system volume until every speaker is 40 dB or more above the noise, but never so loud that it is uncomfortable. The sweeps are digitally capped at −12 dBFS.
